How it works

From first call to court

  1. Share the facts

    Call or message with a short summary of your problem and any deadlines you have received.

  2. Document review

    Notices, agreements, orders and receipts are read so advice is based on papers, not assumptions.

  3. Strategy & notice

    You are told your options, likely timelines and costs. A notice or reply is drafted where needed.

  4. Filing & appearance

    The matter is filed, a vakalatnama is signed and you are kept informed after every hearing.

Questions

Before you call

What should I bring to the first consultation?

Any notice, summons, FIR copy, court order, agreement or receipt you have, along with an ID proof and a short written timeline of events. If you do not have everything yet, call first and you will be told what is needed.

Do you appear in High Courts outside Delhi?

Yes. Matters before other High Courts are arranged as the case requires. Share the court and your next date when you call.

Can the first discussion be on the phone?

The first discussion can usually be done by phone or WhatsApp call. Meetings are by appointment.

How are fees decided?

Fees depend on the court, the type of matter and the work involved. The fee for the first consultation is kept minimal. Fees for taking up a matter are discussed and confirmed before the advocate begins.

Is what I share kept confidential?

What you tell your advocate about your case is kept confidential. Avoid sending sensitive documents over WhatsApp until you have spoken to the advocate.
